How the math works

Commission = item price × your category-and-tier rate. The base is the item price only (Whatnot calls it GMV) — shipping and tax are excluded, which is unusual: most marketplaces charge their percentage on the whole transaction.

Processing = (item price + shipping charged + sales tax collected) × 2.9% + $0.30. Note the base is the opposite of the commission base: everything the buyer paid.

Profit = item price + shipping charged − commission − processing − item cost − your shipping cost. Sales tax is excluded from profit because it was never your money.

FAQ

What is Whatnot's commission in 2026?
It depends on your category and tier. At Standard (under $15K of sales in 4 weeks), most categories pay 8% and coins pay 4%. Sell more and the rate falls — to as low as 6.5% (sports/TCG), 4.5% (fashion), 5% (other collectibles), 3.5% (coins) or 4% (everything else) at the top published tiers. Custom rates as low as 3% exist for the highest-volume sellers by invitation.
Does Whatnot charge commission on shipping or tax?
No. Commission is on the item price only. But the 2.9% + $0.30 processing fee is charged on the full amount the buyer paid, including shipping and tax.
I crossed into a higher tier mid-period. Why is my rate the same?
Rates are locked for the full four-week period. Crossing a threshold lowers your rate from the next period. If your sales drop, your rate rises again the period after that.
Is the 0% commission over $1,500 real?
Yes, as a promotion in select categories (Whatnot lists examples such as trading card games, coins and sports singles): commission is 0% on the portion of a single order's price above $1,500, while processing still applies to the full amount. It's time-bound — confirm it's running in Seller Hub.
What rate do new sellers start at?
Standard. If you joined after Sep 21, 2026, the tiered structure applies from the Monday after you got seller access, and your first periods of sales build the tier you'll get next.
