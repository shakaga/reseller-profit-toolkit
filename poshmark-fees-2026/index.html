Poshmark Fees 2026: $2.95 or 20% — and Nothing Else

The short answer: Poshmark takes exactly one fee. On a sale under $15, it's a flat $2.95. On a sale of $15 or more, it's 20% — you keep 80%. There is no listing fee, no monthly subscription and no separate payment-processing line. Shipping is a flat $6.49 prepaid label paid by your buyer.

Every rate on this page was checked against Poshmark's own Help Center on November 20, 2026. Sources are listed at the bottom, and the change log near the end shows what changed and when.

The one threshold that matters: $15

Poshmark's fee has a cliff built into it — not in the total, but in the rate:

  • At $14.99, the fee is $2.95 (an effective 19.7%).
  • At $15.00, the fee is 20% = $3.00.

The dollar jump is tiny. The real story is below the threshold: the flat $2.95 doesn't scale down, so the cheaper the item, the higher the effective rate. At $10 it's 29.5%. At $5 it's 59%. The two formulas cross at exactly $14.75, the price where 20% equals $2.95 — below that point, the "flat fee for small sales" actually costs you more than the headline 20% would.

Worked examples

Example 1 — a $40 sale

You sell a dress for $40. It cost you $12, and the buyer paid the standard shipping.

  • Commission: $40.00 × 20% = $8.00
  • Payout: $40.00 − $8.00 = $32.00
  • Profit: $32.00 − $12.00 = $20.00

Example 2 — a $12 sale (under the threshold)

You sell a top for $12. It cost you $4.

  • Commission: $2.95 (flat fee — the sale is under $15)
  • Payout: $12.00 − $2.95 = $9.05
  • Profit: $9.05 − $4.00 = $5.05

That $2.95 is a 24.6% effective rate. On cheap items, Poshmark is quietly one of the more expensive platforms — the flat fee, not the 20%, is what eats small sales.

Example 3 — a $120 sale with a free-shipping offer

You sell a bag for $120 and offer free shipping to close the sale. The bag cost you $45. Poshmark deducts the $6.49 label from your earnings because you offered the discount.

  • Commission: $120.00 × 20% = $24.00
  • Payout: $120.00 − $24.00 − $6.49 = $89.51
  • Profit: $89.51 − $45.00 = $44.51

"Free shipping" on Poshmark is never free — it's a $6.49 transfer from you to the buyer, and it comes out of the same payout as the commission.

What doesn't show up in the fee — but still costs sellers

  • Overweight labels. The $6.49 label covers up to 5 lbs. Upgrading costs $5 (5–10 lbs) or $10 (10–15 lbs), paid by the seller.
  • Wrong packaging. Using USPS Priority Mail packaging with the Ground Advantage label triggers a $5 fee deducted from your balance (since October 12, 2025).
  • Texas Seller Fee Tax. Since October 1, 2025, sellers located in Texas have a tax on Poshmark's fee deducted from earnings — Texas treats the fee as a taxable data-processing service. It's small (a percentage of the fee, not the sale) but it will make your payout differ from the calculator by a few cents to a couple of dollars.
  • Promoted Closet. Optional pay-per-click advertising. It's not a sale fee, but it comes out of the same earnings.

The October 2024 experiment — and why the current fee is trustworthy

In early October 2024, Poshmark replaced this commission with a split structure: 5.99% + $1/$2/$3 charged to both buyer and seller. Sellers reported double-digit sales drops, buyers shifted spend from items to fees, and within three weeks Poshmark reversed it — restoring $2.95 / 20% on October 24, 2024. That structure has now held for over two years, which makes Poshmark's fee the most stable in resale — and the easiest to model. If your spreadsheet still has a "Poshmark buyer fee" line from that three-week window, delete it.

FAQ

How much does Poshmark take from a sale? $2.95 on sales under $15; 20% on sales of $15 or more. Nothing else — processing is included.

Is the fee charged on my list price? No, on the final sale price — after the discount or accepted offer that actually closed the sale.

What does Poshmark charge at exactly $15? 20% ($3.00). Poshmark's Help Center phrases it as "$2.95 for sales under $15" and "20% for sales of $15 or more."

Who pays for shipping? Your buyer pays the $6.49 flat rate for a prepaid USPS Ground Advantage label (up to 5 lbs). You pay only if you offer a shipping discount, exceed 5 lbs, or use the wrong packaging.

Does Poshmark charge buyers a fee? Not under the current structure — the buyer-side fee from October 2024 was rolled back after three weeks.

When do I get my earnings? Poshmark releases earnings to your balance after the buyer accepts the order (or 3 days after delivery if they don't respond).
