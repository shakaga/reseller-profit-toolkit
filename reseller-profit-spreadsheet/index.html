Why the fees inside matter more than the columns

Any spreadsheet can subtract cost from price. The hard part is the fee in the middle, because every platform charges differently — and keeps changing:

  • Depop removed its 10% US selling fee in July 2024. Templates that still deduct it understate your Depop profit by a tenth of revenue on every row.
  • Mercari charges 10% — but on the item price plus the shipping the buyer paid.
  • Whatnot replaced its flat 8% with a tiered commission (by category and your 4-week sales) in September 2026.
  • Poshmark is $2.95 flat under $15 and 20% above it — a threshold, not a rate.
  • eBay charges its percentage on the tax-inclusive total in most categories.

Both the free template and the Toolkit use rates verified against the platforms' official pages on November 20, 2026. The Toolkit's Fees Reference tab shows the source for every rate, and Toolkit buyers receive updated sheets when rates change. The free template is a snapshot — accurate on the date above, yours to edit as rates move.

FAQ

Is the free template really free?
Yes — $0 on Gumroad, no card required. It's the front door, not a trial: the inventory and sales logs are fully functional.
Does it work in Excel?
The free template is a Google Sheets file (you can download it as .xlsx, but the Sheets version is the supported one). The paid Toolkit includes a native Excel version.
What does the template not do?
It doesn't import CSV sales reports, calculate a listing price from a target profit, set aside tax money, or update itself when fees change. Those are Toolkit features — see the comparison table above.
How current are the fees?
