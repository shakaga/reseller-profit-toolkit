Now add shipping: put a $7.99 label on that $10 item and the fee becomes $1.80 — 18.0% of the item price. The fee on shipping is Mercari's hidden lever: the cheaper the item, the more the shipping inflates your real rate.

How the math works

Selling fee = (item price + shipping charged to buyer) × 10%. Unlike most platforms, sales tax is not in the base — but shipping is, which most sellers miss.

Buyer Protection = (item price + shipping charged) × 3.6%, paid by the buyer at checkout. It doesn't reduce your payout, but it does raise your buyer's all-in price: on a $40 item with $7.99 shipping, your buyer pays $1.73 extra before tax.

Profit = item price + shipping charged − selling fee − item cost − your shipping cost.

FAQ

How much does Mercari take from a $50 sale?
$5.00 if the buyer pays no shipping — 10% of $50. If the buyer also paid $7.99 shipping, the fee is $5.80, because the 10% covers the shipping too.
Does Mercari charge a payment processing fee?
Not on listings created or updated since January 6, 2025. The old 2.9% + $0.50 processing fee was dropped when the 10% selling fee returned.
Do I pay the Buyer Protection fee?
No — your buyer pays it at checkout (3.6% of item + shipping). It's worth knowing it exists because it raises your buyer's total, but it never reduces your payout.
Is it cheaper to offer free shipping on Mercari?
Fee-wise, slightly: with free shipping there's no shipping in the fee base, so the fee is 10% of the item price alone. But you then pay the label out of the same payout — run both ways in the calculator before deciding.
When do I get paid?
Your balance is released when the transaction completes, after the buyer rates the order. Direct deposit is free; Instant Pay costs $3 per withdrawal if you want it in minutes.
