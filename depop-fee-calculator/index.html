The flat $0.45 is why cheap items sting: on an $8 sale, processing alone takes $0.71 — 8.9% of the price — before you've paid for shipping or the item itself.

How the math works

Processing fee = (item price + shipping charged + sales tax collected) × 3.3% + $0.45. The trap most sellers miss: the percentage applies to the total Depop processes, including the shipping the buyer paid and any tax — not just your item price.

Boost fee = item price × 12%, plus shipping charged × 12% if you shipped on your own label instead of a Depop label. Charged only when a boosted listing sells, and refunded if the sale is refunded.

Profit = item price + shipping charged − processing − Boost − item cost − your shipping cost. Sales tax is excluded from profit because it was never your money.

FAQ

Does Depop still charge a 10% selling fee?
No — not for sellers based in the US, UK or Australia. Depop removed it (US: July 2024). If a calculator or spreadsheet still takes 10% off your Depop sales, it's out of date. Sellers based elsewhere still pay 10%.
What is Depop's payment processing fee in the US?
3.3% + $0.45 per transaction via Depop Payments (Stripe), charged on item price + shipping + applicable taxes.
How much is the Depop Boost fee in 2026 — and is it worth it?
It depends on your margin. At 12%, a boosted sale of a $60 item costs $7.20 on top of processing. Use the calculator above with the Boost box checked and unchecked — if the item wouldn't sell unboosted, compare the boosted profit against $0, not against the unboosted profit.
Who pays the Marketplace fee?
Your buyer, at checkout — up to 5% of the item price plus up to $1. It's worth knowing it exists (it raises your buyer's total), but it never reduces your payout.
Do I get fees back on a refund?
Yes. Depop reverses the selling fee, processing fee and boosting fee automatically on Depop Payments transactions when a sale is refunded.
